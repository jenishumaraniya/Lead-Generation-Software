1. Overview & Commitment

LeadFlow Enterprise ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of personal and enterprise business information. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, processing, usage, and protection of information obtained through our procurement platform, enterprise hardware catalogs, consultation request forms, and related B2B services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver commercial procurement quotations, manage sales interactions, and provide infrastructure recommendations:

  • Commercial & Contact Data: Company name, full name, business email address, direct work telephone number, job title, industry sector, and country/region.
  • Procurement Requirements: Hardware specifications of interest, required quantities, deployment timelines, and stated commercial requirements.
  • Technical & Session Data: IP addresses, anonymized visitor session identifiers, browser characteristics, and page engagement metrics utilized exclusively for lead scoring and qualification.

3. How We Use Collected Data

The information gathered is strictly processed for legitimate commercial business purposes, including:

  • Generating customized enterprise quotations and hardware proposals tailored to your organization.
  • Connecting your request with our designated product category specialist and sales representative.
  • Executing behavioral qualification and automated requirement matching via our ARI Lead Intelligence system.
  • Maintaining an audit log of client communications, meeting notes, and qualification stages.
  • Complying with statutory trade and commercial regulatory requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your enterprise information to third parties. Information may only be shared with authorized infrastructure fulfillment partners, logistics carriers, and verified enterprise vendor networks strictly necessary to fulfill your procurement order under non-disclosure obligations.

5. Data Security & Storage

All enterprise data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256 standards. Our database infrastructure operates in SOC 2 Type II compliant data centers featuring role-based access control (RBAC), multi-factor authentication, and continuous vulnerability monitoring.

6. Your Data Rights

Enterprise representatives may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their contact records at any time by contacting our data protection officer through our compliance channels.
